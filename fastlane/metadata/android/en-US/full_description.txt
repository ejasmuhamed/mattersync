Mattersync by Bytsolv is an independently maintained mobile client for Mattermost servers, with channels, direct messages, threads, file sharing, and voice messages.

- Talk with your team in channels and direct messages
- Follow conversations in threads
- Record and attach AAC .m4a voice messages
- Share files, images, and video
- Connect to your existing team server

A compatible Mattermost server and an account on that server are required. Your server administrator manages accounts, access, and server-side data retention.
